Compatibility

Tested against sixty eight UI mods loaded at once.

- Tabs keep working when another mod's texture is destroyed. Picking the
  RimWorld default preset used to break them.
- The settings page no longer goes blank when another mod throws while the
  tabs are being drawn.
- The plate behind icon buttons stays off portraits and pictures, and steps
  aside when another mod is in charge of colour.
- Better Architect Menu's category groups are drawn with the preset's
  buttons instead of staying flat grey.
- Text outlines are thinner on small text, where a thick ring closed up the
  letters.

Skins

The generated skins are now one group, Legacy. New work goes into the hand
painted ones.
