Recolour any skin

Someone asked whether the colour of a theme could be changed, and it can now.
Three sliders in a preset, under Colour: shift the hue, push or drain the
saturation, lighten or darken. The shading, the bevel and the black outline are
kept, because only the level is scaled and never replaced.

Rotating the hue keeps a skin that was drawn with two inks looking like two
inks: brass over wood stays brass over wood, moved together. That is why it
rotates rather than replacing.

A grey skin has no hue to rotate, so nothing happens to IronPlate or Slate no
matter how far you drag it. Paint over the original colour, the switch under
the sliders, is for those: the hue and the saturation become the colour to
paint with instead of an adjustment to what was already there.

Every preset carries its own, so you can keep the same skin twice in two
colours.

Two new skins

Futuristic, a smooth teal drawn for Electrolize, and EnhancedPixelStone, which
pairs the pixel art with Pixelify Sans and a diamond behind each Architect icon.

The Architect colour shape can be None

The list had ten shapes and no way to have none of them. It is not the same as
turning the colours off: with no shape the category colour still reaches the
button text, if you have that on.
