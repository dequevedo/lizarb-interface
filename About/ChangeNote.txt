Themes became presets

A theme was a folder of textures, and everything else about the look was one
flat set of settings, so two skins could never differ in anything but their
pixels. A preset is the whole look in one click: a texture set, a typeface and
its sizes, the text outline, the background pattern and its strength, and how
the Architect menu is coloured.

That makes PixelStone and PixelStone Fake possible. They are the same pixel
textures. One pairs them with a pixel typeface and a flat background, the other
with ordinary type and a hatch behind it.

Change anything and save it as a preset of your own, from the Presets tab. Your
presets sit under Yours, next to the ones the mod ships.

Nothing you had is lost. Your skin, your font and every setting come across
exactly as you left them; what moved is where they are edited.

Where things went

The settings screen is Presets, Icons, Architect, Animation and Compatibility.
The Text tab is gone: the font, the sizes and the outline are part of a preset
now, and so are the background, the element gap and the Architect colours. The
tabs above hold only what is genuinely global, like the surface switches and
the icon set.

Pixel art is possible now

A theme can declare how many texels it draws per interface pixel, so a small
pixel art texture stays chunky instead of being shrunk into a smooth blur. It
can also ask for its edges to repeat at their own size rather than stretch, the
way Unity's tiled nine slice does, so detail drawn along an edge survives.

Three pixel typefaces ship with the mod: Jersey 10, Pixelify Sans and Jacquard
24, all under the Open Font License.

The Architect menu has room to breathe

Vanilla gives it no margin, so the buttons touch the panel on all four sides
and the info panel and the rotation controls stack flush against it. There is a
gap around and between all three now, and the menu widens to match so nothing
gets narrower. One switch turns it off.

Smaller things

- Point filtering no longer touches the icons. They are drawn small and only
  came out ragged.
- The warning about Architect Icons is gone. The two have composed properly for
  a while and it was describing a bug that was already fixed.
