Twenty themes, split into Squared and Rounded (experimental)

A comment said the rounded themes did not fit RimWorld, and that was fair.
The Theme tab now has two groups, four new squared themes join the list, and
every rounded one is less round than it was.

- New: Slate (nearly square grey stone), Rivet (chamfered steel with corner
  brackets), Vellum (parchment with book corners), Bulwark (chamfered military
  green).
- Copper, Verdant, Bone and Flesh lose the studs in their corners, and Aero is
  a clean bubble with no rim and half the radius.

Icons and chrome

- Twenty two icons for the map toggle row: zones, beauty, room stats, roof,
  heat, fertility, terrain, pollution, home area, auto rebuild, the codex,
  search and the rest.
- The four speed buttons and the window close X get their own glyphs, each on
  a plate in the current theme.
- Architect hotkeys get a keycap behind the letter.
- Need bars are drawn as 9-slice, so their ends follow the theme's corner
  radius instead of fading out.

Everything is drawn at one pixel density

Checkboxes, radio buttons, the slider knob, the bar strips and the background
patterns were authored at half the resolution of everything around them, so
they read softer. They are all at the same density now.

Architect

The colour shape Cascade is replaced by Gradient: a solid square that fades
out across the button. Saved profiles are migrated.
